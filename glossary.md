# Order delivery

Shared terms distinguish the courier's delivery submission from the requester's receipt confirmation.

## Language

**Delivery confirmation receipt**:
The server's acknowledgment of a courier's fixed delivery submission, including its server-received time. A pending receipt has not yet established successful delivery.

**Effective delivery time**:
The server-received time of an ultimately accepted delivery submission, not the phone's time or the time validation finished.

**Requester receipt confirmation**:
The requester's acknowledgment after delivery, which completes the order and requests reward settlement.
